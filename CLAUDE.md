# news.toca.io: working notes for Claude Code

Read this, then `README.md`, `FEED.md` and the tail of `HANDOVER.md`. They are current as of 22 Sept 2026.

## What this is
Static site for Toca's customer stories (home, on navy) and blog (`/blog/`, on paper), generated by `build.py` from `content/articles/*.md` into `site/`. `site/` is gitignored; Netlify builds it from `netlify.toml` on every push. The JSON feeds in `site/` are read by toca.io, toca.health and toca.legal (see `FEED.md`).

## Rules
- Never edit anything in `site/`. Edit `content/`, `build.py`, `css/news.css`, `js/`, `news-cards.js`; then `python3 build.py`.
- `css/site.css` is shared with the sister sites: do not edit it. Override in `css/news.css`.
- No em dashes anywhere in content or code. `build.py` refuses to build if one gets into the output. Use a colon, comma, middle dot or full stop.
- No square brackets in copy unless the text is undecided: that is the brand's convention for placeholder copy and it shows on the site.
- One pink thing per view. Pink is the only colour; everything else is a neutral (or a shade of the navy on the customer page).
- Headings sentence case. British spelling. Do not describe anything as "retro".
- The strip, nav and footer are toca.io's (Mat, 10 Nov 2026): `chrome()` in `build.py` mirrors toca-io-rebuild's `gen.py` `chrome()` and `FOOT` with every link absolute (`IO`) except News, which is `/news/` (works on both hosts), the "chrome" block in `css/news.css` mirrors its `css/io.css`, and `NAV_JS` is its phone menu and its header that slides away while you read down and comes back on any scroll up. News is the current nav item; Customer stories and Blog sit in the sub-nav below. When toca.io's nav or footer changes, change this too.
- The pages are served at www.toca.io/news/ (Mat, 10 Nov 2026): toca.io's `_redirects` proxies `/news/*` to `tocanews.netlify.app`, so what the blog earns in search builds www.toca.io. Every link and asset the pages use is root-absolute under `/news/` (`PATH` in `build.py`); `netlify.toml` rewrites `/news/*` to `/:splat` so the same links work at news.toca.io. Never write a relative link (Netlify cannot add the slash to www.toca.io/news, so it would resolve against www.toca.io); the build refuses one. Never add a redirect with a root-relative target: behind the proxy the browser follows it to www.toca.io. `BASE` (canonicals, sitemap, the feeds' item URLs) stays `https://news.toca.io` until the go-live DNS move; `FEEDS` (feeds, card images, CORS) stays there for good. HANDOVER.md, 10 Nov.
- `news-cards.js` has three rules that must survive any edit: every string via `textContent`; only expected fields, every URL checked against the allow-list; any failure hides the container.
- Figures on customer tiles come only from Toca's own copy (a story's subtitle, toca.io, the brand deck). Do not invent or round.
- Do not add a CMS, a backend, dark-mode toggles, analytics or ads.

## Adding a story
One file in `content/articles/`, named `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`. The schema is the front matter of `content/articles/2026-09-21-writing-for-news-toca-io.md` and the block at the top of `build.py`.
- External (Medium etc): set `external:`; the card links out. Give it `image:` (file in `content/images/`) for the thumbnail.
- Native: no `external:`, write the body in Markdown; images in the body as `![alt](file.jpg)` with a bare file name resolve to `content/images/`. A `.gif` or `.svg` in the body is copied unchanged (animations keep playing); everything else becomes WebP. `image:` (the card) must be a raster file.
- Guides and comparison pieces (the search and AI-answer articles, toca-io-rebuild SEO-GEO.md) go in Learn (Mat, 11 Nov 2026): add `section: learn` and set `tag:` to the category (`Comparisons`, `Guides`; intros in `LEARN` in `build.py`). They live at `/news/learn/<slug>/`, are listed on `/news/learn/` and `/news/learn/category/<tag>/` (after fin.ai/learn), and stay off the blog, the home strip and every feed unless the file also says `feed: true`. Add `author`, `author_role`, `updated` and, once a reviewer agrees, `reviewed_by`; they print a byline and go into the Article markup. A `## Questions` section of `### question` plus answer paragraphs also becomes FAQPage markup. Answer the question in the first paragraph, include a table, end with Questions and Sources.
- Customer story: add `kind: customer`, `customer`, `sector`, and any of `figure`/`figure_label`, `quote`/`quote_by`/`quote_role`, `video` (YouTube ID) /`video_start`, `logo` (ink PNG; recoloured to paper at build), `lead: true` to pin it as the wall's lead, `wide: true` to give its tile two of the three columns.
- From a Medium MHTML: parse with `email`, the article HTML is the `text/html` part, images are the `image/*` parts; place each photo with the section it illustrates; missing dashes in the export are usually em dashes, replace with colons.

## Verify before claiming it works
The pages no longer open styled from `file://`. Run `python3 serve.py` and open http://localhost:8002/news/ (it serves `site/` under `/news/` and, like the proxy, serves a folder without adding the slash). Check `/news/`, `/news/blog/` and any new article, check `pageerror`, check no horizontal overflow at 1400 and 390 wide, check hidden elements are hidden by `offsetParent === null` (not by attribute). Screenshot anything visual and look at it. `node -e "new Function(require('fs').readFileSync('js/x.js','utf8'))"` parses a script quickly.

## Deploy
`git add . && git commit -m "Add: <title>" && git push`. Netlify builds. Nothing else. A push changes news.toca.io and www.toca.io/news/ at once.
