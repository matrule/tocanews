## Who are NextWave?

NextWave isn't your typical consultancy. It's the award-winning specialist alternative to big-brand consultancies that financial services firms trust for market-leading strategy, transformation and AI-enabled automation.

NextWave helps its clients deliver business outcomes rapidly, with specialist transformation teams across front office, risk, data, finance, compliance and operations, and solution consulting and engineering teams on sector-leading platforms and fintech technologies, supported by its own methodologies and accelerators.

## What sets NextWave apart

* **Financial services focus:** deep industry expertise tailored to banking, insurance and financial markets, supporting many of the world's leading global banks, insurance firms, asset and wealth managers, private banks and private equity houses.
* **A connected approach:** NextWave believes in the intelligent orchestration of people and technology to drive the digital revolution.
* **Rapid results:** built to help progressive clients deliver business outcomes quickly.
* **Award-winning excellence:** a recognised leader in financial services transformation.
