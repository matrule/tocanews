**The short answer.** Every GP referral and Advice and Guidance request in England arrives through NHS e-RS, so the choice a Trust faces is what to put around it. If the problem is the time your consultants spend reaching a triage decision, and the admin that follows it, our answer is eTriage by Toca Health, which we build. It puts referrals, Advice and Guidance and consultant-to-consultant requests in one worklist with the patient's hospital record beside each one, and one action updates e-RS, the EPR and the document store together. It has done that at Royal Berkshire NHS Foundation Trust since the beginning of 2024 and is typically £1 per referral. The other products here are built around different jobs: Consultant Connect and Cinapsis around advice between GPs and specialists, Isla around what patients send in, NEC Rego around the GP's decision to refer, Pathpoint around specialty teams, Strata PathWays around discharge and single points of access, and WASP around the whole first outpatient booking. We meet most of them, and we say below where each one is the better choice.

## Start with what you already have

Three things are already in most Trusts, and each deserves a fair look before you buy anything.

**e-RS on its own.** e-RS gets the request to you, and it does that job well. It gives you Referral Assessment Service and Advice and Guidance worklists in its web application, and Advice and Guidance can be converted to a referral where the GP has allowed it. Used on its own, the consultant reads the request in one window and the patient's record in another, and someone keys the outcome into the PAS and the EPR afterwards. For a small service that may be all you need.

**Your EPR's own screens.** Some EPRs take e-RS referrals into their own work queues. If yours gives consultants the whole case on one screen and writes every decision back to e-RS, use it. Ask your supplier four things: whether referrals and Advice and Guidance land in the same queue with their attachments, whether a consultant can convert Advice and Guidance into a referral without leaving the EPR, whether the outcome is written back to e-RS or re-keyed, and when it could be live in your Trust and at what cost.

**The Federated Data Platform.** The FDP sits above your systems and carries national products for waiting list validation, clinic and theatre scheduling, discharge and cancer, with licences funded nationally. Nothing in its published catalogue triages GP referrals or answers Advice and Guidance. It helps you run the waiting list; triage decides what joins it. We have written about the two separately in [Toca and the Federated Data Platform](https://www.toca.io/news/learn/toca-and-the-federated-data-platform/).

## What eTriage adds

eTriage keeps e-RS and your EPR and works on top of them. Nothing changes for GPs, who carry on referring in e-RS exactly as they do now. The difference is on your side:

- **One worklist.** Referrals, Advice and Guidance and consultant-to-consultant requests sit together, each locked to its reviewer so nothing is triaged twice.
- **The whole case on one screen.** The letter, every attachment and the patient's hospital record are in front of the consultant when they decide.
- **One action.** Accept, return, ask for more, or convert Advice and Guidance into a referral with priority and clinic type set. That one action updates e-RS, the EPR and the document store, and clears the case from the worklist. Nothing is re-keyed.
- **Your EPR as it is today.** We use its interfaces wherever they exist. Where they don't, eTriage works the screens under a service account whose permissions you set, and we move onto the interface when it arrives.
- **Inside your boundary.** eTriage runs on your own servers at the Trust or ICB, or hosted in the UK by Toca.

Royal Berkshire reports a 51% reduction in referral admin workload, and 49% of Advice and Guidance requests resolved without a clinic visit. First specialties are typically live within weeks of contract.

## Where each one is the better choice

**Consultant Connect** is the closest to eTriage on the e-RS worklist itself: e-RS Connect lets hospital clinicians work their e-RS lists in a faster screen, and convert Advice and Guidance to a referral. Its real strength is elsewhere. A GP can ring a dial-in number and reach a specialist on a rota, and Consultant Connect can supply consultants from outside your area to answer advice calls or triage a backlog. If you are short of consultant time, that is something software alone cannot give you. On the pages we read, its link to the EPR is an optional export of Advice and Guidance copies.

**Cinapsis** starts with the referrer. GPs, optometrists and community teams raise a structured case and can reach a local specialist by message, phone or video, which suits urgent and same-day advice and services that cover a whole ICB. Its link to the hospital EPR and PAS is an optional module.

**Isla** is the one to see first where the clinical question is answered by a photograph or a video. Patients and clinicians send images and forms, and the same platform runs remote monitoring and patient-initiated follow-up once the patient is on a pathway. eTriage does none of that.

**NEC Rego** works before the referral exists. It opens from the GP's system, checks the referral against local pathways and criteria, and gives commissioners live reporting on what is being referred and why. That makes it a tool an ICB buys for a whole area, and it reaches dentistry and optometry as well as general practice.

**Pathpoint** goes much deeper into a specialty than a triage worklist does. Teledermatology with calibrated images, virtual fracture clinics, theatre planning and digital consent all sit in the same product, and it handles emergency and hospital-to-hospital referrals as well as elective ones.

**Strata PathWays** is built for the other end of the hospital stay and for the spaces between organisations: single points of access and discharge, bed and care home matching, and referrals that cross health, social care and the voluntary sector. Its own pages do not describe specialty triage of outpatient referrals by consultants, or Advice and Guidance.

**WASP RMS** covers more of the outpatient booking process than eTriage does, including partial booking, waiting list management, patient self-booking and reminders, and it keeps clinicians in the EPR screen they already use.

## When to choose eTriage

eTriage is the stronger choice when:

- consultants read the request in e-RS and the record in the EPR, and want both on one screen
- admin teams key each outcome into e-RS, the PAS and the EPR
- Advice and Guidance is rising faster than job plans, and you want it converted to an accepted referral in one step
- requests between your own consultants sit in inboxes, and belong in the same worklist as GP referrals
- your EPR supplier cannot give you an interface yet
- the data has to stay inside your own network
- you want the bill to follow the work, at a price per referral

Nothing stops a Trust running eTriage beside any of the others. A telephone advice line for GPs, image capture from patients or a discharge hub does a different job, and the triage decision still has to reach e-RS and the EPR.

## Six questions to put to every supplier, us included

1. **Which requests reach the worklist?** Referral Assessment Service referrals, Advice and Guidance, and requests from inside the hospital, with every attachment.
2. **What does the consultant see beside the request?** The referral alone, or the patient's hospital record as well.
3. **What happens after the decision?** Which systems are updated by that one action, and which are still keyed by hand.
4. **Can Advice and Guidance become an accepted referral without leaving the screen?** With priority and clinic type set.
5. **Where does it run?** A public cloud, a UK data centre, or your own servers.
6. **What does it cost at your volumes over three years?** Per specialty, per patient, per appointment or per referral, with set-up and integration counted.

## Questions

### What software can triage NHS e-RS referrals and Advice and Guidance for a hospital Trust?

Several products take e-RS referrals and Advice and Guidance onto a worklist of their own. eTriage by Toca Health is built for the consultant's triage decision in secondary care and writes it to e-RS, the EPR and the document store in one action. Consultant Connect's e-RS Connect and Cinapsis SmartReferrals also work e-RS worklists, alongside telephone and message advice for GPs. Isla, NEC Rego, Open Medical's Pathpoint, Strata PathWays and WASP RMS each take e-RS referrals as part of a wider product.

### Do we need anything on top of e-RS?

Not always. e-RS on its own will get every request to your consultants, and for a small service that can be enough. Trusts add a triage tool when consultants are working across e-RS and the EPR, when outcomes are keyed in more than once, or when Advice and Guidance volumes outgrow the time set aside for them.

### Can our EPR do this instead?

Sometimes. If your EPR takes referrals and Advice and Guidance into one work queue, shows the whole case and writes every decision back to e-RS, use it. Many Trusts are waiting on a supplier's roadmap or an unfunded change request, and eTriage is for the time in between: it uses the EPR's interfaces where they exist and its own screens where they don't.

### Does the Federated Data Platform triage referrals?

Nothing in the FDP's published catalogue triages GP referrals or answers Advice and Guidance. Its products help a Trust manage the waiting list once it exists. eTriage records the decision that puts a patient on the list, or keeps them off it.

### What are the alternatives to Isla for NHS referral triage?

For the consultant's triage worklist, eTriage, Consultant Connect's e-RS Connect and Cinapsis. For referral checking in primary care, NEC Rego. For a specialty's whole workflow with clinical images, Open Medical's Pathpoint. If the pathway depends on photos and monitoring from patients, Isla itself is built for that.

### How much does referral triage software cost?

Suppliers charge in different units. On their G-Cloud 15 price lists, Consultant Connect's e-RS Connect starts at £20,000 a year per specialty, Cinapsis at £18,000 for one specialty, Pathpoint at £27,000 a year for one specialty team, Isla at £1.70 per appointment, and NEC Rego is priced per registered patient with a £50,000 minimum. eTriage is typically £1 per referral in NHS acute and general hospitals, with no other costs.

### Who makes the triage decision in eTriage?

A clinician, every time. eTriage brings the request and the record together and carries the decision to each system; it does not make the decision.
