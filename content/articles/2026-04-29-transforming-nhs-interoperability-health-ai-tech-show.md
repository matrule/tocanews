![The Toca.health team at Stand 2, Health AI Tech Show](2026-04-29-health-ai-tech-show-stand-2.jpg)

## The interoperability dilemma: why AI needs modern data flow

For many NHS Trusts, the biggest barrier to adopting AI isn't a lack of interest: it's legacy infrastructure. Patient data is frequently trapped across disconnected Electronic Patient Record (EPR) systems, legacy databases and department-specific software.

Attempting to bolt advanced AI tools onto fragmented data structures creates friction, increases security risks and inflates costs.

To deliver genuine value, AI cannot exist as an isolated point solution. It must be paired with robust integration architecture that streamlines data flow across entire patient pathways.

## Three pillar approach: apps, integration, and LLMs

At the Health AI Tech Show, we demonstrated how bringing **apps, integration, and Large Language Models (LLMs) into a single platform** allows Trusts to overcome interoperability hurdles safely and cost-effectively:

* **Vast reductions in clinician triage time:** by automating the ingestion, sorting, and pre-processing of incoming referral data, clinicians spend significantly less time on manual administrative tasks and more time on direct patient care.
* **Reducing the cost of referrals:** streamlining patient pathways removes operational drag, lowers administrative overhead, and prevents costly delays in care delivery.
* **Safe, auditable, on-premise enterprise AI:** safety and data sovereignty are non-negotiable in healthcare. Deploying enterprise AI securely, often on-premise, means Trusts keep total control over sensitive patient data while every automated decision stays fully auditable.
