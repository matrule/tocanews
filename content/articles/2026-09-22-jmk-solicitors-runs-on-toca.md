JMK Solicitors is Northern Ireland's number one personal injury and car accident claims firm, handling thousands of cases a year and more than double the caseload of any competitor.

Toca sits between the firm's case management system and the services a case depends on: the medical agencies, the MOJ Portal and Open Banking. Routine steps are automated and the fee-earners see one view of a case. The firm reports 25% fewer files at risk of write-off.

Keith Sutton and James Swash visited the team in Newry in August 2026 to plan the next year's work; [that visit is written up on the blog](https://medium.com/@toca.io/future-proofing-the-practice-inside-our-visit-to-jmk-solicitors-e0159e63878c).
