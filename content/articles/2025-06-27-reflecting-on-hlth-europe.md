Just a week ago, the team was buzzing through the halls of the RAI Convention Centre in Amsterdam, immersed in the vibrant energy of **HLTH Europe 2025**. It was an incredible experience, packed with insightful discussions, groundbreaking innovations and invaluable networking opportunities that truly underscored the rapid evolution of the European healthcare landscape.

One theme resonated louder than any other throughout the conference: **interoperability** (apart from AI, of course!). From keynotes to intimate roundtables, the urgent need for seamless data exchange across disparate healthcare systems was consistently at the forefront. Discussions revolved around the European Health Data Space (EHDS) and its transformative potential, but also the very real challenges of integrating new digital health solutions into existing, often fragmented, legacy infrastructures. The consensus was clear: without true interoperability, the promise of AI and efficient patient pathways will remain largely unfulfilled.

![The hall at HLTH Europe 2025, RAI Convention Centre, Amsterdam](2025-06-27-reflecting-on-hlth-europe-1.jpg)

## Where Toca.health comes in

This is precisely where [Toca.health](https://toca.health) truly shines, and why our time at HLTH Europe was so important for us. We had such compelling conversations demonstrating how our low-code platform is directly tackling these interoperability hurdles.

In a sector often plagued by data silos and complex integrations, Toca empowers healthcare organisations to:

* **Bridge fragmented systems:** we showcased how Toca can connect disparate electronic health records (EHRs), patient administration systems (PAS) and other vital platforms, unifying data that was once locked away.
* **Streamline data flow:** through our automation and integration capabilities, we can create intelligent workflows that ensure information flows securely and efficiently between care providers, patients and even across national borders, supporting initiatives like the EHDS.
* **Accelerate innovation adoption:** many solutions presented at HLTH Europe are brilliant in isolation but struggle with implementation because of integration complexity. Toca provides the foundational layer to connect these tools with existing IT ecosystems quickly, drastically reducing deployment times and accelerating time to value.
* **Empower clinical and administrative staff:** by automating repetitive data tasks and providing intuitive interfaces built on connected data, Toca frees clinicians to focus on patient care and helps administrative staff work more efficiently.

Our team had numerous productive conversations with healthcare leaders, providers and fellow innovators who share our vision for a more connected and efficient healthcare ecosystem. It was particularly exciting to discuss how Toca's agility and speed can unlock the potential of data for secondary use, driving research and improving public health outcomes, all while maintaining stringent data security and privacy.

## Interoperability is the enabler

HLTH Europe reinforced our belief that interoperability isn't just a technical challenge; it's a fundamental enabler for the future of healthcare. We left Amsterdam more energised than ever, committed to using Toca.health to help our partners navigate this complex landscape and truly transform care delivery.
