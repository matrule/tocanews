*Hint: it's not always your CMS.*

Law firms today are under immense pressure to deliver faster, more efficient service. Yet, even with the right tools in place, many firms find themselves bogged down by unseen friction. You've invested in a robust case management system (CMS), perhaps a finance tool, and even a client portal. So why does legal work still feel like it's crawling?

## The challenge

It's not always about having the wrong tools, but rather how those tools, and your workflows, interact (or don't). Clunky processes, disconnected systems and the relentless tide of administrative overhead are silently holding firms back, pulling focus away from high-value legal work and affecting client delivery.

![Top 5 bottlenecks slowing down law firms](2025-06-11-what-s-really-slowing-your-legal-work-down.jpg)

## The problem

We've identified five common workflow friction points that often go unnoticed, even in firms using advanced case management:

1. **Disconnected systems:** your CMS, finance tools and client portals often operate in silos. This forces your team to enter the same information into several systems. It's slow, repetitive and a massive drain on productivity, taking valuable time away from actual casework.
2. **Workflows still need chasing:** even when tasks are digitised, they often don't move forward without someone actively pushing them. Teams spend valuable time "nudging" processes along, leading to stalled cases and wasted staff time.
3. **Too many update requests:** clients, understandably, want to know what's happening with their cases. When updates aren't readily available, they resort to calls and emails, turning your legal staff into customer service agents instead of progressing casework.
4. **Billing is always behind:** a common bottleneck is the disconnect between completed work and invoicing. Finance teams often don't hear about billable activity in time, leading to delays in invoicing and revenue recognition, and unnecessary back-and-forth.
5. **Too much time spent on admin overall:** these friction points add up to an excessive amount of time spent on routine administrative tasks. That hurts margins and limits your firm's capacity to grow without adding staff.

## The solution

The good news? You don't necessarily need to rip out and replace your core systems to fix these issues. The key lies in optimising your existing environment and automating those unnoticed friction points.

Imagine a world where:

* **Information flows seamlessly** between your CMS, finance tools and client portals, eliminating repetitive data entry.
* **Workflows trigger automatically**, so tasks progress without constant manual reminders.
* **Clients receive real-time updates**, reducing their need to chase your team and freeing your staff for higher-value work.
* **Billing kicks off automatically** as soon as work is completed, for faster revenue collection.
* **Routine admin tasks are automated**, allowing your team to focus on complex legal challenges and strategic work.
