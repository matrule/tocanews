The Toca team attended the **Thames Valley Tech & Innovation Awards 2026**, joining some of the region's most forward-thinking businesses, founders and innovators for a fantastic evening of networking and celebration.

Being shortlisted alongside such a talented group of local companies is a massive win for our team and a strong reminder of how fast the Thames Valley tech scene is growing.

Plus, surviving a high-energy awards ceremony in the middle of a scorching summer heatwave seems to be fast becoming our unofficial company tradition!

![The Toca team at the Thames Valley Tech & Innovation Awards 2026](2026-07-10-thames-valley-tech-innovation-awards-2026.jpg)

## Highlights and standout moments

Beyond the chance to catch up with familiar faces and make new connections, the night was a showcase of exceptional local talent:

* A huge congratulations to **Sachin Sunil** for taking home the *Rising Star in Tech Award*. Recognising emerging talent is vital for keeping our local ecosystem competitive and creative.
* A special shoutout to **Sam Haynes** and **Rachel Stedman** from **Wireless Logic**, who took home the trophy in our category this time. It's great to stand shoulder-to-shoulder with companies pushing the boundaries of technology.
* From digital healthcare to enterprise automation, the sheer variety of innovations coming out of the Thames Valley proves that world-class tech hubs don't just exist in major capital cities.

## Stronger together

Building complex, high-impact technology requires a supportive community. Events like these highlight the importance of local networks, regional sponsors, and media partners who champion innovation across the South East.

A huge thank you to the organisers at **The Business Magazine, South East** and **Insider Media**, as well as the event sponsors **R3vamp**, **Crowe UK**, **Knights** and **BDO UK**, for hosting such a memorable night.
