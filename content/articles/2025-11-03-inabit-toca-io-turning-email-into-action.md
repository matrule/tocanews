## Introducing Mail Flow

InaBIT Mail Flow transforms how firms handle email. Instead of letting messages sit in inboxes, Mail Flow routes them to the right teams, prioritises urgent items, assigns tasks and manages attachments automatically.

By connecting email directly to workflows, Mail Flow turns everyday communications into structured, actionable processes, saving time and reducing errors.

## What Mail Flow can do for your team

**Automated routing.** Messages are sent intelligently to the correct team, department or individual, so nothing is delayed or overlooked.

**Workflow orchestration.** Routine messages trigger actions like opening new files, drafting acknowledgements, or flagging items for review.

**Seamless integration.** Mail Flow works with existing systems, including case management, document storage and compliance tools.

**Task assignment and priority filtering.** Incoming messages are assigned based on rules and priority, keeping teams focused on what matters most.

**Document flow management.** Attachments are automatically categorised and sent to the correct workflow stage, whether for billing, client records or disclosure.

**Performance insights.** Track volumes, response times and workload trends to keep improving efficiency.
