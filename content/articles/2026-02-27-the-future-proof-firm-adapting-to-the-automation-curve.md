Our CEO, Mat Rule, recently joined [**Savant Recruitment Experts**](https://www.savantrecruitment.com/) at the Royal Air Force Club in London to address this precise inflection point, exploring how law firms can adapt to an increasingly automated landscape.

During the session, Mark Sheldon presented a striking metric that set the tone for the entire discussion: **research suggests that 82% of a newly qualified lawyer's current tasks could be automated by AI today.**

Within twelve months, that total could reach 100%.

Whether that 100% projection feels like forward-looking hyperbole or an imminent reality, the underlying direction of travel is undeniable. Traditional, routine tasks such as initial document review, contract analysis, regulatory checking and data entry across case management systems (CMS) are rapidly being taken over by intelligent automation.

![The panel at the Royal Air Force Club, hosted by Savant](2026-02-27-savant-panel-raf-club.jpg)

## Rethinking the value of junior talent

This shift doesn't mean human lawyers are becoming obsolete. Instead, it signals a massive evolution in where a lawyer's real value lies:

* **From manual data processing to strategic thinking:** when automation handles repetitive, rules-based tasks, junior legal professionals can shift their focus toward high-level strategy, client relationship management and complex problem-solving much earlier in their careers.
* **Auditability and compliance matter more than ever:** as automated systems take on larger portions of legal workflows, firms must ensure that these tools remain fully transparent, auditable and compliant with strict regulatory standards.
* **Operational agility as an advantage:** firms that integrate flexible, secure automation into their existing architectures will outpace those anchored to legacy, manual processes.

## Navigating the shift

Unlocking the real value of AI in legal operations requires software designed to bridge the gap between static systems and modern, automated workflows. Moving fast cannot come at the expense of control; total operational clarity is non-negotiable.
