In healthcare delivery, **time** is the ultimate resource. Yet for clinicians across the NHS, significant portions of the working day are consumed by manual administrative processes: sorting incoming referrals, navigating fragmented software systems and re-entering data across legacy databases.

Asking clinicians to manage these administrative bottlenecks directly affects patient care, extends waiting lists and inflates the cost per referral across trusts.

To tackle these challenges head-on, the Toca.health team, represented by **Seb Clancy** and **Catherine Tilson**, recently attended the **InterSystems UKI Data Summit** in Birmingham. Exhibiting in the Startup Zone, the team demonstrated how combining modern digital infrastructure with enterprise-grade AI can give clinicians their valuable time back.

![Seb Clancy and Catherine Tilson on the Toca.health stand at the InterSystems UKI Data Summit](2026-09-16-modern-digital-infrastructure-in-healthcare-nhs-referrals.jpg)

## The interoperability barrier in referral management

For most NHS trusts, the barrier to efficient triage isn't a lack of clinical skill: it is data fragmentation. When a referral enters a trust, the necessary patient information often lives across disparate electronic patient record (EPR) systems, legacy databases and e-Referral Systems (e-RS).

Traditional approaches attempt to solve this by purchasing point solutions: standalone software tools that handle one specific task. However, introducing isolated tools often adds complexity, creating new data silos and requiring clinicians to navigate yet another interface.

Solving this requires a unified platform approach that connects three core capabilities in a single operational layer: **custom application creation, seamless system integration, and enterprise-grade large language models (LLMs).**

## Three key outcomes for NHS partners

By bringing application building, integration and AI under one umbrella, trusts can transform referral pathways while maintaining strict safety standards. At the Data Summit, we highlighted three outcomes we are delivering for our NHS partners now:

* **Accelerated triage times:** intelligent data automation ingests, sorts and pre-classifies incoming referral data instantly. With pre-structured information in front of clinicians, waiting and assessment times drop significantly, shifting waiting lists from years to weeks.
* **Cost efficiency across pathways:** manual referral processing carries a high administrative overhead. Digitising and streamlining these workflows lowers the overall cost per referral, so trusts can optimise budget allocation across frontline services.
* **Clinical safety and total auditability:** AI in healthcare cannot exist as a black box. Our platform ensures that AI deployments are safe, fully auditable and built around healthcare compliance standards, giving clinical teams full transparency into how data is processed.
