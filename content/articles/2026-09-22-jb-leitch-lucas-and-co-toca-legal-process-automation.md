When automating complex legal workflows, standard low-code and process automation tools often fall short. Property management litigation and leasehold legal services involve intricate, high-stakes processes that demand precision, security and absolute reliability. Attempting to force rigid, generic software onto legacy case management systems (CMS) usually results in costly IT overhauls, operational downtime and frustrated fee-earners.

To overcome these challenges, **JB Leitch Ltd**, one of the UK's leading specialist property law firms, partnered with legal process consultancy **Lucas and Co** and enterprise development platform **Toca**.

Together, we've built a seamless, flexible digital foundation that automates complex processes directly via CMS APIs, achieving deep operational integration without disrupting core underlying IT infrastructure.

![Driving legal process automation: Toca partners with JB Leitch Ltd, delivered alongside Lucas and Co](2026-09-22-jb-leitch-lucas-and-co-toca.png)

## The transformation

This project represents a modern blueprint for legal process automation, uniting specialised technology, expert process design and forward-thinking firm leadership.

### Zero infrastructure disruption

By leveraging Toca's enterprise low-code platform, JB Leitch's new business processes are built, optimised and automated independently of underlying IT systems. Integrating directly through CMS APIs means core operations continue to run smoothly behind the scenes, ensuring zero downtime or friction for staff and clients.

### Tailored workflows designed around real legal practice

Technology is only as effective as the process strategy behind it. Lucas and Co provided the deep legal consultancy needed to map out complex real-world workflows. Instead of bending legal practice to fit rigid technical limitations, the automation was built specifically to mirror the high standards JB Leitch's operations demand.

### An AI-ready foundation built on clean data

Deploying enterprise AI effectively requires structured, reliable data and clear operational baselines. Toca's integration layer provides JB Leitch with clean data feeds and transparent workflows, establishing the exact digital foundation required to deploy future AI capabilities safely and measure return on investment accurately.

## Leadership insights

As **Matt Jarvis**, Managing Director at JB Leitch, noted regarding the project's impact:

## Future-proofing the firm

The legal sector is evolving rapidly, driven by changing regulations, shifting client expectations and the rise of practical AI. By deploying a permanent, flexible automation and integration capability, JB Leitch isn't just solving today's operational bottlenecks: they have built an adaptable framework that will allow the firm to scale and innovate for years to come.
